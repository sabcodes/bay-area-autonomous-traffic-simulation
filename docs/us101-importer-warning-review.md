# US-101 importer warning review

**Review date:** 2026-10-03
**Scope:** Four `netconvert` warnings noted in the [network audit](us101-network-audit.md#relevant-importer-warnings). This review checks the captured OSM source, generated SUMO connections, and dated aerial/map context. It does not certify the design against construction plans or field conditions.

## Evidence limits

The project OSM extract was downloaded from Overpass on **2026-09-22**; provenance is in [`us101_corridor.osm.provenance.json`](../data/osm/us101_corridor.osm.provenance.json). The four Google Maps aerial captures were taken on **2026-09-24**. They show context and pavement, but are not surveyed plans and do not establish lane-level geometry or signal phase intent. The saved SUMO `.net.xml` records the imported topology and lane connections, not their correctness against as-built plans.

SUMO's [OSM import documentation](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Networks/Import/OpenStreetMap.html) says lane-to-lane links are generally guessed from the imported data, that complex junction clusters flagged by warnings need manual checking, and that incomplete turn-lane tags may be interpreted as through lanes. The [PlainXML junction-joining documentation](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Networks/PlainXML.html#reasons-for-joining-node-clusters) explains that clusters with parallel incoming edges may be filtered out. These warnings therefore identify model-review points; they are not evidence by themselves that a physical movement is missing.

## Overall recommendation

None of the four warnings has enough plan-level evidence to call its junction
geometry or lane connections survey verified. A route-file check confirms that
`348360455`, `690599564#0`, `941192341`, and `19146053#0` are absent from
the fixed H0 route set. The three Shoreline/Mathilda cases have been reviewed
and are **accepted as documented M2 approximations**: the OSM movement exists,
the corresponding directed SUMO links are present, the available aerials show
the expected interchange context, and no supported correction was identified.
The warnings remain visible in the rebuild log. West Maude/SR-237 is outside
the US-101 mainline/H0 acceptance area and remains a local-network warning.
No geometry change, warning suppression, or forced junction join is supported
by the available evidence.

The 2026-10-04 rebuild retained the raw OSM import alongside the corrected
Mathilda/Rengstorff network. The raw pass again reported the Shoreline
`348360455` sharp turn (7.99 m), the `690599564#0` parallel-outgoing cluster,
and the `941192341` parallel-incoming cluster. Reimporting the corrected
network still reported a sharp turn at `348360455` (8.18 m). The lane patch
does not target these three movements. The branch ledger confirms their
connected route paths, while the H0 run checks only its fixed route set.

These warnings do not block M2 under the project's practical acceptance bar.
If a later scenario is sensitive to one of these movements, check its route
behavior and current lane use before interpreting that scenario. Seek detailed
plans only if that check reveals a material contradiction that cannot be
settled from available sources. Caltrans District 4 lists
[as-built records as requestable records](https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-4/d4-popular-links/district-4-records-request),
but obtaining them is not an M2 completion requirement.
